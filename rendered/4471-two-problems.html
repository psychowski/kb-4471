The problems

  1. parser. The shared parser reads every date month first. Day-first tenants get month and day flipped. This is the bug the old page describes, and it covers most of the reports.
  2. hint. The booking-v3 form tells people to type MM/DD/YYYY. Day-first tenants on that form do what the hint says, and the form reads it their way. The parser fix does not touch this. The hint text needs its own fix. Intake tagged about half of these parser, so the tag cannot tell the two apart.

A few reports read as a one-day move (desk-notes.json). They stay counted under the cause their tenant gives them, and the table footnotes them.

How the numbers are made

The table runs causes.ts on every build, takes out the oneDayMove reports, and counts them as their own problem. Nobody types a number into this page.

Why the old merge hid this

Decision 0001 merges on subject alone. Subject lines say "wrong day" for all two problems. The decision says engineering merges later; here nobody did.
